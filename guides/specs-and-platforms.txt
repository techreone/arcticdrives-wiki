<p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> Arctic Drive needs a GTX 1650, 4 GB of RAM and 8 GB of storage at minimum, with an RTX 3060 and 8 GB recommended. It is PC-only on Steam, also on Xbox Game Preview, supports crossplay but not cross-save, and has partial controller support plus wheel support.</p>
<p>The game is not a hardware monster. It is a physics-heavy co-op sandbox built on a winter map, and the official specs are modest for a 2026 release. The bigger compatibility questions are input and platform, not frame rate: whether your wheel works, whether your friend on Xbox can join, and whether your save follows you. The <a href="/guides/early-access-roadmap" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">Early Access review<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> covers whether the game is worth installing; this page covers whether it will run.</p>

<p>The storage figure is small because the map is procedural and the asset set is compact. The RAM minimum is the one to watch: 4 GB is enough to launch, not enough to run a browser, voice chat and the game at once. If you stream or keep Discord open, treat 8 GB as the real minimum.</p>
<h2 id="will-it-run-on-a-gaming-laptop">Will it run on a gaming laptop?</h2>
<p>Probably, with caveats. A GTX 1650 laptop is the minimum GPU, and laptop versions are slower than the desktop card the spec names. Expect low-to-medium settings, a capped frame rate and fans. The recommended RTX 3060 is a safer target if you want the game to look like the screenshots.</p>
<p>The game also has a history of performance complaints in reviews, especially in multiplayer. A machine that hits the recommended spec is not a guarantee of a smooth four-player session; the host's connection and the number of players matter as much as the GPU.</p>
<h2 id="does-arctic-drive-support-controllers-and-wheels">Does Arctic Drive support controllers and wheels?</h2>
<p>Steam lists <strong>Partial Controller Support</strong>, not full. That usually means a gamepad works for driving but may need a keyboard or mouse for menus, inventory or crafting. Do not buy a couch setup assuming every screen is navigable with a pad.</p>
<p>Steering wheels are a different story. The v0.23.0 patch added wheel support with clutch, manual gears and calibration, and the launch patch fixed force feedback so it releases when you tab out. Reviews still complain that individual gear bindings and some shifters are not supported. If you own a direct-drive wheel and a separate shifter, test your bindings before planning a serious session.</p>
<div class="callout callout-tip"><div class="callout-title">Steam Deck</div><div class="callout-body"><p>The game defaults to Medium settings on Steam Deck. It runs, but the small text and inventory management are the weak points, not the frame rate.</p></div></div>

<p>The Xbox version is a Game Preview build, which is the console equivalent of Early Access. It costs slightly more and may update on a different cadence. The Steam version gets the PC-specific advantages: Steam Cloud, Family Sharing and the eventual mod support listed on the roadmap.</p>
<h2 id="can-steam-and-xbox-players-play-together">Can Steam and Xbox players play together?</h2>
<p>Yes. Xbox lists cross-platform co-op, and the game has an in-game crossplay toggle. The host controls the session, and the other players join through the game's multiplayer flow. Crossplay does not merge saves: a Steam host's world stays on Steam, and an Xbox player's progress stays on Xbox.</p>
<p>If your group cares about one persistent base, pick a platform and stay there. If you just want to drive with friends, crossplay is enough. The <a href="/guides/co-op-roles" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">co-op roles guide<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> covers how to split the crew once everyone is in the truck.</p>
<h2 id="what-about-vr-mac-and-linux">What about VR, Mac and Linux?</h2>
<p>No VR mode has been announced. The store lists Windows only, with no Mac or Linux build. Steam Proton may run it on Linux, but that is community compatibility, not an official platform. Do not buy it for a Mac unless you have a Windows partition or a cloud gaming plan.</p>
<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 id="is-8-gb-of-ram-enough">Is 8 GB of RAM enough?</h3>
<p>For the recommended spec, yes. For running the game, voice chat and a browser on the same machine, 16 GB is the comfortable choice.</p>
<h3 id="does-the-game-support-ultrawide">Does the game support ultrawide?</h3>
<p>No official ultrawide support has been announced. The first-person dashboard and UI are designed for 16:9, so expect letterboxing or stretched elements on wider screens.</p>
<h3 id="can-i-transfer-my-steam-save-to-xbox">Can I transfer my Steam save to Xbox?</h3>
<p>No. There is no cross-progression, and Steam Cloud only syncs between Steam machines.</p>
