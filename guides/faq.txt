<p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> It is Arctic Drive, though the store itself jokes about "Artic Drive." You can play solo, crossplay works between Steam and Xbox, and saves are per platform with Steam Cloud on PC. Lag and lost progress are real Early Access risks, so save after every stop.</p>

The rest of this FAQ covers the questions that decide whether you buy, how you play and what to do when the truck, the lobby or the save file lets you down.</p>

<h2 id="is-it-arctic-drive-or-artic-drive">Is it Arctic Drive or Artic Drive?</h2>
<p>The correct name is <strong>Arctic Drive</strong>. "Artic" is a common misspelling because the second c is easy to drop in speech and typing. The official store page acknowledges it directly: "Arctic Drive (or Artic Drive for some)." Search results for both spellings lead to the same Steam appid, 4009490.</p>
<p>Use "Arctic Drive" in store searches, wishlists and forum posts. Use "Artic Drive" only if you are searching for a specific post or video that used the typo. The game is not a different title, and there is no separate "Artic Drive" release.</p>
<h2 id="can-you-play-arctic-drive-solo">Can you play Arctic Drive solo?</h2>
<p>Yes. Steam lists Single-player alongside Online Co-op, and the entire loop works alone: drive, load, repair, recycle, craft, sleep. What changes is the workload. In a crew, the driver stays in the seat while someone else loots; solo, you are the driver, the loader, the mechanic and the map reader.</p>
<p>Solo is harder but quieter. You can leave the truck running while you loot a cabin, because there is no passenger to complain, but you also have no one to watch your back or fetch a part. The <a href="/guides/first-20-minutes" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">first 20 minutes guide<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> is written for solo play; the <a href="/guides/co-op-roles" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">co-op roles guide<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> covers the crew version.</p>
<h2 id="does-arctic-drive-have-crossplay">Does Arctic Drive have crossplay?</h2>
<p>Yes. Xbox lists cross-platform co-op, and the game has an in-game crossplay toggle. A Steam host can play with Xbox players, and the session is controlled by the host's settings. Crossplay does not merge saves or progress. If you want one persistent base, choose one platform for the group.</p>
<h2 id="why-is-arctic-drive-multiplayer-laggy">Why is Arctic Drive multiplayer laggy?</h2>
<p>Early Access netcode is the short answer. Reviews report lag, ping and packet loss, including crashes when joining a session. The game simulates a lot of physics: cargo, parts, vehicle deformation and player movement. That is expensive to synchronize, especially when the host is on a home connection.</p>
<p>What helps:</p>
<ul>
<li>Play with the lowest-ping host.</li>
<li>Keep the crew to two or three players if four is unstable.</li>
<li>Avoid hosting while downloading or streaming.</li>
<li>Join before the host leaves the garage, not mid-blizzard.</li>
<li>Use voice chat instead of text for split-second calls.</li>
</ul>
<p>What does not help: blaming the driver. The <a href="/guides/co-op-roles" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">co-op guide<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> has a callout system that reduces the number of things that need to be said twice.</p>
<h2 id="how-do-saves-work-and-can-you-lose-them">How do saves work, and can you lose them?</h2>
<p>The game supports <strong>Save Anytime</strong> and <strong>Steam Cloud</strong>. v0.13.8 changed game-over behavior so saves are no longer deleted when the run ends, and v0.23.2 fixed cargo falling under the car after loading a save. Those are meaningful fixes, but they do not make saves indestructible.</p>
<p>Risk factors:</p>
<ul>
<li>Crashing during a save.</li>
<li>A host disconnecting while a client is loading.</li>
<li>Steam Cloud conflicts between two PCs.</li>
<li>Corrupting a save with a mid-air or mid-crash quit.</li>
</ul>
<p>The practical rule is to save after every major stop, not every 10 minutes. Save at a cabin or garage, not on a slope, and keep one backup vehicle at a fixed base. If you are playing co-op, let the host save and wait for the confirmation before anyone closes the game.</p>
<h2 id="does-arctic-drive-support-controllers">Does Arctic Drive support controllers?</h2>
<p>Steam lists <strong>Partial Controller Support</strong>, so a gamepad works for driving but some menus and inventory actions may still need a keyboard or mouse. Steering wheels are supported since v0.23.0, including clutch, manual gears and calibration, but individual gear bindings and some shifters remain a complaint. The <a href="/guides/specs-and-platforms" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">specs and platforms page<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> has the full input and platform breakdown.</p>
<h2 id="how-much-does-arctic-drive-cost">How much does Arctic Drive cost?</h2>
<p><strong>$19.99 on Steam</strong>, with no launch discount. The Xbox Game Preview version is listed at about <strong>$21</strong>. There is no Game Pass announcement, no deluxe edition and no preorder bonus. The price is the price; if that changes, the store page will show it first.</p>
<h2 id="is-arctic-drive-like-the-long-drive">Is Arctic Drive like The Long Drive?</h2>
<p>Players compare it to <strong>The Long Drive</strong> with a survival layer and co-op, and that is a fair starting point. The differences are the physical cargo, the repair and crafting loop, the four-player crew and the colder survival pressure. It is not a racing game with a story; it is a drive where the truck is the base.</p>
<h2 id="what-should-i-do-first">What should I do first?</h2>
<p>Install the battery, keep one jerrycan and one repair kit, drop the decorations, and drive to a cabin before dark. Do not loot every shelf on the first stop. The full sequence, including what to throw away, is in the <a href="/guides/first-20-minutes" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">beginner guide<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a>.</p>
<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 id="is-arctic-drive-on-game-pass">Is Arctic Drive on Game Pass?</h3>
<p>No Game Pass version has been announced. It is sold on Steam and Xbox Game Preview.</p>
<h3 id="does-it-have-a-story-mode">Does it have a story mode?</h3>
<p>It has scenarios and a sandbox. The roadmap lists story as part of the Early Access phase, but the launch build is driven by the survival loop rather than a campaign.</p>
<h3 id="can-i-play-with-mods">Can I play with mods?</h3>
<p>Not yet. Mod support is listed in the Expeditions roadmap phase, with no date.</p>
