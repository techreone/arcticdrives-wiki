<p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> A camper is a home you can drive; a trailer is a warehouse you tow; a fixed garage is the cheapest workshop you can build. Start fixed, add a trailer for resupply, and only move to a full-time camper when your route is long enough to justify the fuel.</p>
<p>The truck is already a shelter, but a base is where the loop stops being reactive. You need a powered bench, a recycler, a dry bed and a parking spot that does not trap you in a drift. The official feature list offers two routes: hook up a trailer or an RV for a mobile base, or build out a fixed shelter with a generator. The <a href="/guides/recycling-and-crafting" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">crafting guide<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> covers the workshop; this page covers where to put it.</p>

<h2 id="what-counts-as-a-base-vehicle-in-arctic-drive">What counts as a base vehicle in Arctic Drive?</h2>
<p>Three things qualify:</p>
<p>Route choice decides whether the base is reachable at all; the <a href="/guides/route-reading" class="guide-action-capsule" data-link-type="internal-capsule">route reading guide<span class="capsule-arrow">→</span></a> covers ice, bends and whiteouts.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>The truck</strong> — seats, storage, heat and the only way out. It is the base until you build something better.</li>
<li><strong>The camper or RV</strong> — a driveable shelter with a bed and interior space. It can be parked as a forward base.</li>
<li><strong>The trailer</strong> — a towed cargo body with shelves and a ramp. It is not a shelter by itself, but it can carry the workshop.</li>
</ul>
<p>A fixed base is any shelter you improve: a cabin, garage or stash room with a generator, heat, lights and a bench. The distinction that matters is not wheeled versus static. It is whether you can leave without packing.</p>

<p>The cheapest setup is a fixed garage with a generator and a bench, plus the truck for day trips. The most flexible is a camper. The worst of both is a trailer packed with everything you own and no fixed place to sort it.</p>
<div class="callout callout-tip"><div class="callout-title">The two-base rule</div><div class="callout-body"><p>Keep one fixed workshop and one mobile kit. The fixed base holds the recycler, spare parts and overflow cargo; the mobile kit holds one repair kit, one jerrycan, food and warm clothes.</p></div></div>
<h2 id="how-do-you-set-up-a-camper-as-a-mobile-base">How do you set up a camper as a mobile base?</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Park level and nose-out.</strong> A camper on a slope slides, and a blocked exit turns a shelter into a trap.</li>
<li><strong>Check the heater before you unpack.</strong> Heat is the reason to use a camper instead of a tent.</li>
<li><strong>Secure loose items.</strong> The v0.23.2 patch fixed cargo falling under the car when loading a save; it did not make shelves self-closing.</li>
<li><strong>Keep the driving kit in the cab.</strong> Repair kit, torch, food and one jerrycan should never be buried under base cargo.</li>
<li><strong>Mark the spot.</strong> If you leave the camper, note the landmark so you can find it in a whiteout.</li>
</ol>
<p>The v0.23.0 patch fixed players being ejected from the camper at high speed in co-op and improved the shelves. That makes the camper more usable at speed, not indestructible. Slow down before a bend with passengers in the back.</p>
<h2 id="how-do-you-use-a-trailer-without-losing-it">How do you use a trailer without losing it?</h2>
<p>A trailer is a force multiplier on a known road and a liability on an unknown one. Use it for base moves and resupply, not for exploration.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Attach before loading.</strong> A loaded trailer is harder to line up with the hitch.</li>
<li><strong>Use the ramp.</strong> The v0.23.0 trailer ramp saves time and saves your back.</li>
<li><strong>Hand-pull the last metre.</strong> Get out, use the hitch, and position the trailer without forcing the truck into a tight spot.</li>
<li><strong>Drop it before a steep descent.</strong> A trailer that starts steering the truck is already too heavy for the grade.</li>
<li><strong>Check the door after every stop.</strong> A trailer door that opens on the road empties your base.</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not put your only repair kit in the trailer. If the trailer is lost, the truck should still be able to fix itself.</p>
<h2 id="what-does-a-fixed-base-need">What does a fixed base need?</h2>
<p>A useful fixed base has five things:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Heat</strong> — a stove, heater or generator-powered room.</li>
<li><strong>Power</strong> — generator fuel for lights and the recycler.</li>
<li><strong>A bench</strong> — crafting and research in one place.</li>
<li><strong>Storage</strong> — a stash room for parts, food and overflow cargo.</li>
<li><strong>A clean exit</strong> — a parking spot you can leave without reversing into a drift.</li>
</ol>
<p>The v0.23.2 patch fixed the stash room's generator, heat and lights, which makes the prefab garage a stronger first base than it was at launch. Check that the generator actually runs before you move your whole stock inside.</p>
<h2 id="when-should-you-switch-from-fixed-to-mobile">When should you switch from fixed to mobile?</h2>
<p>Switch when the drive from your base to the content is longer than the content itself. If you spend 20 minutes commuting and 10 minutes looting, a camper pays for itself. If you spend 10 minutes commuting and an hour crafting, the fixed base is cheaper.</p>
<p>A good rule: stay fixed until you have a full repair kit, a spare wheel and a known fuel stop on the far side of the map. Then move the mobile kit, not the whole base.</p>
<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>
<h3 id="can-you-sleep-in-a-trailer">Can you sleep in a trailer?</h3>
<p>The trailer is cargo space, not a heated shelter. Sleep in the truck, the camper or a cabin. A trailer parked next to a cabin is useful storage, not a bedroom.</p>
<h3 id="does-a-camper-use-the-same-fuel-as-a-truck">Does a camper use the same fuel as a truck?</h3>
<p>It burns fuel when driven and needs repairs like any vehicle. That continuous cost is why a fixed base is cheaper for short routes.</p>
<h3 id="can-you-tow-a-trailer-with-a-camper">Can you tow a trailer with a camper?</h3>
<p>The game supports trailer hookups, but a camper plus trailer is a long, heavy combination. Test it on a straight road before you take it into the hills.</p>
